About

I am a Geoinformatics researcher with interdisciplinary expertise in Precision Agriculture, Remote Sensing, GIS, Climate Analytics, and Machine Learning applications for sustainable agriculture. I recently completed my Master’s degree in Geoinformatics at Shivaji University, Kolhapur, and hold a Bachelor’s degree in Agriculture.

My research interests focus on developing data-driven and geospatial approaches for crop monitoring, pest forecasting, environmental analysis, and climate-resilient agricultural systems. I have worked on integrating satellite remote sensing, weather parameters, field observations, and spatial analytics to support precision farming and decision-making.

During my research internships at ICRISAT and NRSC–ISRO, I contributed to projects involving pest population forecasting, vegetation analysis, and agricultural monitoring using geospatial technologies and time-series modeling techniques. My work has involved the application of ARIMAX, SARIMA, LSTM, RNN, and other machine learning models for agricultural and environmental datasets.

I am interested in research areas related to:

  • Agricultural AI and Machine Learning

I am seeking PhD and research opportunities where I can contribute to innovative research in geospatial technologies, artificial intelligence, and sustainable agricultural systems. Deep Learning-Based Maize Leaf Disease Classification Using ResNet-18

Built a deep learning-based image classification system using ResNet-18 (PyTorch) to detect maize leaf diseases. The model classifies leaf images into Blight, Common Rust, Gray Leaf Spot, and Healthy categories, achieving ~99% training accuracy and ~97–99% test accuracy. Implemented data preprocessing, model training, evaluation, and inference.

Deep Learning · CNN · ResNet · Computer Vision

Rearing Protocol for Major Insect Pests in Grain Legume and Dryland Cereal Crops

Entomology · Pest Management · Agriculture

AgroSense-AI: Agentic AI System for Crop Stress Monitoring Using Satellite Data – Ahmednagar District, Maharashtra

AgroSense-AI is an agent-based geospatial AI framework designed to monitor crop stress using Sentinel-2 satellite data and Google Earth Engine. The system calculates NDVI values to assess vegetation health and classify crop conditions. A modular AI pipeline consisting of data acquisition, analysis, reasoning, and advisory agents generates interpretable outputs for agricultural decision support. Results are visualized in ArcGIS Pro to support crop monitoring in drought-prone regions like Ahmednagar district.

GeoAI · Google Earth Engine · Sentinel-2 · NDVI · ArcGIS Pro · AI Agents

Assessment of Climate Variability Impacts on Sugarcane Production in Kolhapur District using DSSAT Crop Simulation Model and GIS Techniques

This study analyzes sugarcane productivity in Kolhapur district using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, GIS analysis, and DSSAT crop simulation modeling. NDVI-based vegetation monitoring was integrated with weather, soil, and management data within DSSAT to simulate crop yield. Spatial yield maps were generated to evaluate climate variability impacts on sugarcane productivity.

DSSAT · Remote Sensing · GIS · NDVI · Crop Modeling · Climate Analysis

Development of GeoAI Agents for Automated Satellite-Based Agricultural Monitoring and Watershed Intelligence

Developed custom ArcGIS Pro tools using Python and AI APIs to automate geospatial analysis workflows including crop health monitoring, satellite index computation, watershed delineation, and environmental monitoring. The system integrates Sentinel-2 remote sensing data, GIS processing, and AI-assisted automation to enable large-scale agricultural and environmental analysis.

GeoAI · ArcGIS Pro · Python · Remote Sensing · Watershed Analysis
